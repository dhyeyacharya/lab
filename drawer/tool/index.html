Tracks and connectors

–

Every container gets one closed track running all the way round under its base. Where a slanted container meets a neighbor, a strap connector bridges the gap between their tracks on the shortest line. Straight walls, Dome or Standard, sit flush and need none.

    Printable files

    –

    The ZIP holds one STL per part (millimetres), plan.pdf to print, and layout.json, the hand-off to the Grasshopper definition. Every part has its number and name debossed underneath.

    Print notes

    FDM
    • Print containers rim-down. The track and the label come out on top, crisp, and Standard containers need no supports.
    • A Dome container rim-down needs a little support under the flat middle of its bowl.
    • Connectors print flat, strap down, with no supports. Their label is on the strap's underside. Each button's slotted ring stands free on the bed except over the strap's neck, so it can flex; PETG gives the softest click.
    • Each file is a set of closed shells; your slicer merges them into one part.
